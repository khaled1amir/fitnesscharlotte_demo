FACILITY STANDARDS

CULTURE & GYM RULES

To protect our community and equipment, strict enforcement policies apply ($20 fine or membership termination for violations).

01. Respect Members & Staff

Zero tolerance policy for bullying or harassment of any kind.

02. Door Security Policy (24/7 Safety)

Never hold the door for anyone. Every member must scan their own key fob. Report piggybacking to 704-622-7201.

03. Equipment Courtesy

Always re-rack weights in their designated spots and wipe down equipment after use.

04. Attire & Posing Protocol

Shirts and shoes are required on the main floor. Shirt removal and bodybuilding trunks are allowed only inside the dedicated Posing Room on the Full Access side.
